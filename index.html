Fresh Towels.
Smarter Hospitality.

AeroFresh is a towel hanger with a built-in dryer. It senses how damp the towel is and blows air through the towel to dry it. Guests get fresh towels, and hotel staff do less work.

The Problem Statement

Hotel towels stay damp after use, and a regular towel rack only holds them. It does not dry them.

01

Guests

Guests want fresh and comfortable towels during their stay.

02

Hotel staff

Hotel staff handle many towels every day, which adds to their work.

03

Regular racks

Regular racks cannot sense or dry the towels.

How it works

Five steps, fully automatic

Hang the towel. AeroFresh handles the rest — sensing, Control, drying and confirming.

01

Hang

Place the damp towel on AeroFresh.

02

Sense

Sensors check the humidity and temperature.

03

Control

The controller picks the right drying mode.

04

Dry

A small fan blows air through the hollow arms into the towel.

05

Finish

The fan slows down or stops when the towel is ready, and a light turns on.
